Program: Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)

LIHEAP provides financial assistance to income-qualified households to help pay heating and cooling costs. The program is designed for households that spend a high portion of their income on energy bills and may face difficulty maintaining safe indoor temperatures.

LIHEAP may provide bill payment assistance, crisis intervention during utility shutoff risk, and limited weatherization services to improve home comfort and efficiency. Assistance amounts depend on household income, size, and energy costs.

This program is especially relevant during extreme heat or cold conditions when energy usage increases and utility bills rise. LIHEAP helps reduce immediate energy burden and supports vulnerable households in maintaining safe living conditions.
